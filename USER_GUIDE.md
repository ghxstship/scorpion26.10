# User Guide

## Getting Started

### Creating an Account

1. Click "Get Started" or "Sign Up"
2. Enter your email and create a strong password (minimum 12 characters with uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters)
3. Verify your email address
4. Complete your profile

### Logging In

1. Click "Login"
2. Enter your email and password
3. Note: After 5 failed login attempts, your account will be locked for 15 minutes for security

### Resetting Your Password

1. Click "Forgot Password" on login page
2. Enter your email address
3. Check your email for reset link
4. Create a new strong password

## Browsing Products

### Finding Products

1. Navigate to "Programs" in the menu
2. Use filters to narrow your search:
   - Category
   - Price range
   - Type (digital, physical, service, subscription)
3. Click on a product to view details

### Product Details

Each product page shows:
- Full description
- Pricing
- Features included
- Reviews and ratings
- Purchase or booking options

## Making Purchases

### Adding to Cart

1. Select product
2. Choose quantity (if applicable)
3. Click "Add to Cart"
4. Continue shopping or proceed to checkout

### Checkout Process

1. Review cart items
2. Enter shipping information (for physical products)
3. Enter payment details (Stripe secure checkout)
4. Review and confirm order
5. Receive order confirmation email

### Payment Methods

We accept:
- Credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex)
- Debit cards
- Digital wallets (via Stripe)

## Booking Services

### Making a Booking

1. Navigate to service page
2. Click "Book Now"
3. Select date and time
4. Choose duration
5. Complete payment
6. Receive booking confirmation

### Managing Bookings

View your bookings in Account > Bookings:
- Upcoming bookings
- Past bookings
- Reschedule or cancel (subject to policy)

### Cancellation Policy

- Cancel up to 24 hours before: Full refund
- Cancel within 24 hours: 50% refund
- No-show: No refund

## Troubleshooting

### Can't Log In

- Verify email and password
- Check for account lockout (wait 15 minutes)
- Try password reset
- Clear browser cache

### Payment Failed

- Verify card details
- Check card balance
- Try different payment method
- Contact your bank

### Page Not Loading

- Refresh the page
- Clear browser cache
- Try different browser
- Check internet connection
