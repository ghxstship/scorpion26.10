# 456AF Content & Brand Voice Guide

**Brand:** 456 Athletics & Fitness (@456af)  
**Created:** November 2025

---

## BRAND VOICE FORMULA

**75% Drill Sergeant Bootcamp** - Direct, motivating, no-BS intensity  
**20% Mean Girls** - Sharp, clever, unapologetically confident  
**5% Gen Z Dry Humor** - Self-aware, subtle irony, meme-adjacent wit

---

## TONE PRINCIPLES

1. **Be direct, never apologetic**
   - ❌ "We'd love to help you on your fitness journey"
   - ✅ "Stop lurking. What's your excuse?"

2. **Confidence without cringe**
   - ❌ "We're the best fitness program in the world!"
   - ✅ "This isn't for everyone. That's the point."

3. **Humor that punches up, not down**
   - ❌ "Unlike those other lazy gyms..."
   - ✅ "It's literally $30. That's less than your streaming subscriptions you don't even watch."

4. **No corporate speak or fluff**
   - ❌ "Join our welcoming community of fitness enthusiasts"
   - ✅ "Join a community that actually shows up. No flakes. No excuses."

## WRITING GUIDELINES

### Do's
- Use contractions (you're, don't, it's)
- Write like you talk
- Call out excuses directly
- Use specific numbers ($30, 6 slots, 3 cohorts)
- Emphasize scarcity and exclusivity
- Be self-aware about pricing

### Don'ts
- Use corporate jargon ("synergy", "holistic", "journey")
- Apologize or soften the message
- Make empty promises
- Use excessive emojis
- Write long paragraphs (keep it punchy)
- Sugarcoat limitations
