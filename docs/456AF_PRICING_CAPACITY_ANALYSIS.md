# 456AF Pricing & Capacity Analysis

**Brand:** 456 Athletics & Fitness (@456af)  
**Analysis Date:** November 2025  
**Trainer Work Week:** 30 hours max

---

## CAPACITY PLANNING (30-Hour Work Week)

### Assumptions
- **Admin/Planning Overhead:** 6 hours/week (20% of total time)
- **Available Training Time:** 24 hours/week
- **Session Prep/Follow-up:** 15 minutes per client session
- **Onboarding Time:** 2 hours per new client (one-time)

---

### Basic_AF - Community Access Only
**Model:** Self-serve community platform  
**Trainer Time:** 2 hours/week (community management, content posting)  
**Maximum Capacity:** Unlimited (scalable digital platform)  
**Recommended Pricing:** $30/month (current)

**Rationale:** Community-driven, minimal trainer involvement. Price point removes friction for entry-level customers.

---

### Strong_AF - Virtual Group Training
**Model:** Virtual group sessions + programming  
**Session Structure:**
- 3 group sessions/week @ 60 minutes each = 3 hours
- Group size: 15-20 clients per session
- Programming time: 2 hours/week (shared across all clients)
- Check-ins: 15 min/client/month = 5 hours/month (1.25 hours/week for 20 clients)

**Weekly Time Investment:**
- Group sessions: 3 hours
- Programming: 2 hours  
- Monthly check-ins: 1.25 hours
- **Total:** 6.25 hours/week

**Maximum Capacity:** 60 clients (3 groups of 20)  
**Current Pricing:** $300/month + $150/in-person session  
**Recommended Pricing:** $350/month + $200/in-person session

**Rationale:** 
- Industry benchmark for virtual group training: $250-$400/month
- In-person session premium reflects travel/facility costs
- Price increase accounts for quality programming and community value

---

### Rare_AF - Private Personal Training
**Model:** 1-on-1 personalized coaching  
**Session Structure:**
- 2 sessions/week @ 60 minutes = 2 hours training time
- Prep/follow-up: 30 min/week per client
- Custom programming: 1 hour/week per client
- Weekly video analysis: 30 min/week per client
- **Total per client:** 4 hours/week

**Maximum Capacity:** 6 clients (24 hours ÷ 4 hours per client)  
**Current Pricing:** $500/month + $250/in-person session  
**Recommended Pricing:** $800/month + $300/in-person session

**Rationale:**
- Industry benchmark for elite 1-on-1: $600-$1,200/month
- 8 sessions/month = $100/session (highly competitive)
- Recommended $800/month = 8 sessions @ $100/session
- In-person premium: $300 reflects exclusivity and direct access
- Scarcity model (6 slots max) justifies premium pricing

---

### Elite_AF - Residential Program (Ultra-Premium)
**Model:** Immersive destination experience  
**Session Structure:**
- 1 client at a time (exclusive)
- Full-time dedication during residency
- 3 clients maximum per year
- Duration: Typically 30-90 days per client

**Maximum Capacity:** 3 clients/year  
**Current Pricing:** $30,000/month  
**Recommended Pricing:** $50,000/month

**Rationale:**
- Industry benchmark for residential training: $40,000-$100,000/month
- Includes: Housing, meals, 24/7 access, travel, full lifestyle management
- Comparable to celebrity trainer rates
- Scarcity (3 slots/year) + exclusivity = premium positioning
- Current $30k undervalues the offering
