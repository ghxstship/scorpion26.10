## Getting Started

### Creating an Account

1. Navigate to the homepage
2. Click **Sign Up** in the navigation menu
3. Enter your email address and create a password
4. Check your email for a verification link
5. Click the verification link to activate your account

### Logging In

1. Click **Login** in the navigation menu
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click **Sign In**

**Forgot your password?**
- Click "Forgot Password" on the login page
- Enter your email address
- Check your email for a reset link
- Create a new password

## Account Management

### Viewing Your Profile

1. Log in to your account
2. Click your avatar in the top right
3. Select **Account** from the dropdown

### Updating Your Information

1. Go to **Account** → **Settings**
2. Update your:
   - Full name
   - Email address
   - Password
   - Notification preferences
3. Click **Save Changes**

### Managing Notifications

Control which emails you receive:

- **Order confirmations** - Receive updates about purchases
- **Booking reminders** - Get reminders before appointments
- **Newsletter** - Stay updated with new content
- **Marketing emails** - Receive promotional offers

## Products & Services

### Browsing Products

1. Click **Products** in the navigation
2. Browse available products and services
3. Use filters to narrow your search:
   - Product type (Digital, Physical, Service, Subscription)
   - Price range
   - Availability

### Purchasing a Product

1. Click on a product to view details
2. Click **Add to Cart**
3. Review your cart by clicking the cart icon
4. Click **Checkout**
5. Enter payment information
6. Click **Complete Purchase**

**Payment Methods:**
- Credit/Debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express)
- Secure payment processing via Stripe

### Viewing Your Purchases

1. Go to **Account** → **Purchases**
2. View all your past orders
3. Download receipts
4. Request refunds (within 30 days)

## Bookings

### Making a Booking

1. Click **Services** or **Booking** in the navigation
2. Select a service
3. Choose an available date and time
4. Fill in any required information
5. Click **Book Now**
6. Complete payment if required

### Managing Your Bookings

1. Go to **Account** → **Bookings**
2. View upcoming and past bookings
3. Actions available:
   - **Reschedule** - Change date/time (up to 24 hours before)
   - **Cancel** - Cancel booking (cancellation policy applies)
   - **Add to Calendar** - Download calendar event

### Booking Reminders

You'll receive reminders:
- 24 hours before your appointment
- 1 hour before your appointment
- Confirmation email immediately after booking

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### Can't Log In

**Problem:** "Invalid email or password" error

**Solutions:**
1. Check your email address for typos
2. Ensure Caps Lock is off
3. Try resetting your password
4. Clear browser cache and cookies
5. Try a different browser

#### Email Verification Not Received

**Solutions:**
1. Check your spam/junk folder
2. Wait 5-10 minutes for delivery
3. Request a new verification email
4. Add noreply@yourdomain.com to your contacts
5. Contact support if still not received

#### Payment Failed

**Solutions:**
1. Check your card details are correct
2. Ensure sufficient funds are available
3. Try a different payment method
4. Contact your bank
5. Contact support for assistance

#### Booking Not Available

**Solutions:**
1. Refresh the page to see latest availability
2. Try a different date/time
3. Check if the service is still offered
4. Contact support for custom scheduling
